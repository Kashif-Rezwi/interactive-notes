# Experience Principles

1. **Orient before complexity.** State the question, outcome, prerequisite, and route through the material.
2. **Reveal structure.** Teach relationships, not disconnected facts.
3. **Invite prediction.** Ask learners to reason before showing an answer or animation.
4. **Make interaction consequential.** Controls must alter a meaningful model or decision, not merely decorate the page.
5. **Explain feedback.** A correct/incorrect signal without rationale does not teach.
6. **Support multiple representations.** Pair prose, notation, examples, visuals, and accessible alternatives when they clarify different aspects.
7. **Expose limits.** State assumptions, edge cases, uncertainty, and common misconceptions.
8. **Respect cognitive load.** Prefer progressive disclosure, hierarchy, and one new idea per meaningful step.
9. **Design for recovery.** Learners can reset, review prior ideas, and revisit explanations without penalty.
10. **Assess transfer.** Include at least one opportunity to apply the idea beyond its introductory example.
