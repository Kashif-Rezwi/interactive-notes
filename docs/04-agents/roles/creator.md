# Role card: Stage 1 Creator

**Status:** Experimental<br>
**Owner:** Repository maintainer<br>
**Review by:** 2026-11-04<br>
**Activation scope:** Bounded manual private pilots<br>
**Reference roles represented:** Parser; Concept Extractor; Teacher; Curriculum Planner; Visualization Planner; Experience/Interaction Planner; Generator

## Purpose and trigger

Transform an authorized source into a bounded candidate through source understanding, learning design, experience specification, and generation. Activate only after intake authorizes the intended use.

## Inputs and outputs

Consumes an approved source package, relevant policies, learning objective, and evaluation intent. Produces extraction evidence, concept model, learning plan, experience specification, candidate, and a complete generation handoff.

## Authority and prohibited actions

May propose instructional and interaction choices within approved scope. Must not approve source rights, assign a final quality score, waive defects, alter review evidence, or release its own candidate.

## Tools, data, and communication channels

Uses authorized source material, templates, approved prompts where available, and handoff packets. Declares assumptions, gaps, and model limitations.

## Quality checks and evaluation measures

Check source anchors, outcome alignment, accessibility intent, specification completeness, and candidate readiness. Measure defect recurrence and score movement after targeted revisions.

## Failure modes and escalation owner

Escalate unsupported claims to the Steward, domain disagreements to the Reviewer, and scope or risk questions to the Coordinator.

## Separation-of-duties constraints

The Creator cannot be the sole Reviewer or release authority for the same candidate. A same-person review is non-independent and blocks public release.

## Retirement or activation-change criteria

Split this profile when repeated pilot evidence shows that source analysis, pedagogy, visualization, or generation needs a separate accountable specialist.

## Validation and promotion trigger

Three completed private pilots plus the review evidence required by the review policy.
