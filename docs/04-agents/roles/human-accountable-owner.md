# Role card: Human Accountable Owner

**Status:** Experimental<br>
**Owner:** Repository maintainer<br>
**Review by:** 2026-11-04<br>
**Activation scope:** Bounded manual private pilots and public-distribution decisions<br>
**Reference roles represented:** Human Accountable Owner

## Purpose and trigger

Hold non-delegable accountability for source authorization, risk acceptance, policy exceptions, and public release. Activate for every source-authorization decision, material escalation, and proposed public learner release.

## Inputs and outputs

Consumes source manifests, review evidence, gate audits, exception requests, and escalation packets. Produces explicit decisions, constraints, and review dates.

## Authority and prohibited actions

May accept or reject residual risk within the documented policy. Must not fabricate technical evidence, erase defects, or delegate final accountability.

## Tools, data, and communication channels

Uses decision records, source manifests, evaluation evidence, and documented escalation packets.

## Quality checks and evaluation measures

Verify scope, evidence, alternatives, residual risk, mitigation, and a follow-up date. Measure timely, traceable decisions and unresolved exception age.

## Failure modes and escalation owner

Pause work and seek appropriate domain, accessibility, legal, or privacy expertise when evidence is insufficient for a responsible decision.

## Separation-of-duties constraints

Public release requires independent Reviewer evidence in addition to this role's approval. Human accountability does not substitute for specialist review.

## Retirement or activation-change criteria

Add delegated human owners only when responsibility, access, and escalation authority can remain explicit and auditable.

## Validation and promotion trigger

Three completed private pilots plus the review evidence required by the review policy.
