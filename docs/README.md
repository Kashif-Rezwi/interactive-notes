`docs/` is the authoritative operating manual for Learning OS. Its numbered directories encode a recommended reading order and metadata inheritance boundary:

1. `00-foundation` — why the project exists and the principles that constrain it.
2. `01-product` — learner value, artifact intent, and boundaries.
3. `02-system` — conceptual architecture and contracts.
4. `03-workflows` — governed execution and the quality loop.
5. `04-agents` — accountable roles and coordination.
6. `05-prompts` — versioned behavioral assets.
7. `06-evaluation` — measurable quality and release gates.
8. `07-memory` — durable organizational learning.
9. `08-observability` — lineage, logging, retention, and incident learning.
10. `09-operations` — repeatable playbooks and cadence.
11. `10-governance` — standards, versioning, reviews, and ADR policy.
12. `11-roadmap` — maturity stages, risks, decisions still open, and implementation gates.
13. `adr` — immutable major decisions.

Read the [repository entry point](../README.md) first. Do not treat a document as authoritative merely because it is under `docs/`: status, owner, and linked decision determine its operational authority.
